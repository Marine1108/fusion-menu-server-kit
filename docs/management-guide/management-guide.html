Running a Fusion server

The day-to-day handbook for your staff team: the admin panel, reports, moderation, the anticheat, managing players and staff, departments, and keeping the server healthy. Setup and every setting are in the Server Owner's Guide.

Staff ranks

Every staff member has one or more roles. A role has a priority, and the higher one always wins.

RolePriorityCan do
Player0Everyday features. Every player has it.
Moderator50Admin panel, reports, kick, warn, freeze, revive, heal, teleport, spectate, noclip, logs
Admin100Everything admin: bans, money, items, jobs, settings, roles below their own
Owner1000Everything, always. Can't be removed.
  • Staff can only act on people below them. Kick, ban, warn, freeze, kill and log out only work on players with a lower priority, and you can only hand out roles below your own. Owners are exempt.
  • The owner can rename these, change what they allow, and add roles like Senior Moderator, LSPD Command or Donator.

Every location setting has a Here button: stand where the thing should be and click it.

Daily routine

  • Reports first. F10 › Reports. Claim anything open, even if you can only reply later.
  • Anticheat. F10 › Anticheat: look at new detections. Most are clear-cut; check anything marked Alert or Log before acting.
  • Logs. F10 › Logs: scan for large money or item changes and anything staff did that looks off.
  • Dashboard. Player count, staff on duty, uptime. Use Delete empty vehicles if the city is cluttered.
  • Owners: F10 › Updates for a waiting Fusion update, and the console for script updates or warnings.

Reports

Players send reports from M › Help & Reports, /calladmin or /report. Staff with report access hear a sound and see a red badge.

  • Claim it so others know it's yours. The player is told who is helping.
  • Go to the player or the report location, or Bring them to you.
  • Message them without leaving what you're doing.
  • Close with a short note. The player sees it, and it's kept in the logs.

Tip: claim before you go. Two staff arriving at the same report wastes both your time.

Moderation

Use the lightest step that fixes the problem. Every action is logged with your name.

StepHow
TalkMessage them (Players › Message) or go to them
WarnPlayers › Warn, or /warn. Warnings stay on their record
FreezeStops them moving while you sort it out
SpectateWatch without being seen; Backspace stops
KickPlayers › Kick, or /kick. They can rejoin
Ban/ban <id> <hours> <reason> or Players › Ban. 0 hours = permanent
  • Always give a reason. The player sees it, and it's what the next staff member reads.
  • Ban someone who already left from F10 › Bans with their identifier.
  • Unban from F10 › Bans. Check the reason and who banned them first.

Anticheat

It runs on the server and watches for god mode, teleporting, speed hacks, entity and explosion spam, blacklisted weapons and models, injected scripts and known cheat menus.

  • Each detection does what the owner set: Log, Alert (staff get a sound), Kick or Ban.
  • F10 › Anticheat lists every detection with the player and the details. Open the player, ban them, or delete a false positive.
  • A legit player was caught? Unban them in Bans, delete the detection, and tell the owner which detection fired so it can be loosened.
  • Staff with anticheat.bypass (admins by default) are never flagged.

Managing players

  • Online: F10 › Players. Open someone for revive, heal, teleport, inventory, money, items, job, gang and roles, plus their identifiers and warnings.
  • Offline: F10 › Characters edits names, money and job of any character.
  • Refunds after a bug: check Logs for what they had, then give it back with Players › money / items (or /givemoney, /giveitem). Note it in the report.
  • Stuck players: /goto, /bring, or send them to character select from Players.
  • Lost car: it's usually in the impound or a garage. My Vehicles shows where.

Managing the team

  • Add staff: F10 › Roles & Permissions › pick a role › Members › Add online player, or Add by identifier for someone offline.
  • Remove staff: the same list, remove them. Their access ends straight away.
  • Permissions have three buttons: Allow, Inherit (the role it builds on decides) and Deny. The most specific rule wins, and deny beats allow.
  • New role (e.g. LSPD Command): create it, pick what it builds on, set its priority below the people who manage it, allow only what it needs.
  • Other scripts' permissions (an MDT, CCTV): add the script's permission name as a rule on the role.
  • Locked out? In the server console: fusion_role <your id> superadmin.

Jobs & departments

  • Jobs tab: grades, pay and which grades are bosses. Bosses hire, promote and fire in game from M › Job.
  • /setjob <id> <job> <grade> sets anyone's job; /setgang their gang.
  • PIT rules belong to each department: its command staff set them in M › Emergency › Department PIT Rules (allowed, seconds before PIT, top speed). Admins can set every department in Settings › Emergency Vehicles.
  • Jail: police and staff use /jail <id> <minutes> <charges> or M › Jail; /unjail releases early.

Homes, weather & alerts

  • Housing (F10 › Housing): Add a home here standing outside the real door; then Set garage here sitting in a car; Entrances for more doors outside (back door, balcony); Go inside, then Exits, for the ways out inside. Homes without a garage show No garage yet.
  • Residents lists who lives in a building; Evict puts a home back on sale and moves its cars to a public garage.
  • Weather: M › Weather & Time, or /weather, /time, /blackout. Severe events: /weatherevent tornado (also thunderstorm, flood, hurricane, blizzard; stop ends it). Everyone gets a warning first.
  • Alerts: F10 › Alerts sends an emergency or AMBER alert to every phone. AMBER plates go onto the police BOLO list.
  • Announcements: /announce <message> or the Dashboard.

Server upkeep

  • Settings changes apply straight away for everyone; no restart needed.
  • Clean-up: F10 › Server removes empty vehicles, stray NPCs and props.
  • Restarting a resource on the Server tab lasts until the next restart. Use the switches in Settings for permanent changes.
  • Script Configs (owner): change other scripts' settings, start them with the server (the green / red button), and update free scripts from GitHub. Every change is backed up.
  • Vehicle packs: F10 › Sirens & Carcols fixes clashing sirens and liveries; the Handling tab tunes addon cars.

Updates & backups

  • Fusion updates (F10 › Updates): new versions download and check themselves. Pick Install (players get a countdown), When the server is empty, or At this time. If a version doesn't start, the old one comes back by itself, and Undo is in the history.
  • Free scripts update themselves at start or while the server is empty. Edited config files are kept, with the new defaults beside them as .new.
  • Back up settings before big changes: F10 › Settings › Export. Import restores them.
  • Back up the database regularly (phpMyAdmin › Export, or your host's panel). Characters, money and homes live there.
  • Never delete the [fusion] folders to update by hand: that throws away pictures and media. Copy new files over them.

When something breaks

  • Write down what happened: who, when, what they did, the exact message. Screenshots help.
  • Support file: F10 › Support › Create support file (or fusionsupport in the server console). Passwords, keys and IP addresses are blanked out.
  • Open a ticket on the Fusion Menu Discord with the support file: discord.gg/kVRqwDPsnJ
  • A script misbehaving after an update? Look for .new files in its folder, or use Undo update in Script Configs.
  • Something wrong straight after a Fusion update? F10 › Updates › Undo.
