How to play on a Fusion server

Everything you need on your first night in the city: the keys, the menu, your phone, cars, homes, jobs and how to get help. Every section can be copied straight into Discord.

First join

  • The first time you join, a short server guide shows before you pick a character. Open it again any time with /guide or M › Server Guide.
  • Create a character: name, date of birth, nationality. New characters usually open the appearance editor so you can dress them.
  • Pick where to spawn from the spawn screen: city locations, your last location, or one of your homes once you own one.
  • Change character later with M › Switch Character or /logout.

Every server is set up differently. Owners can rename things, change keys, turn features off or limit them to certain jobs. If something here is missing on your server, it has been switched off.

Change any of these in GTA Settings › Key Bindings › FiveM (the names start with Fusion). Prompts on screen always show the key you picked.

In menus

↑ ↓ move, ← → change a value, Enter select, Backspace back, Esc close. You can keep walking and driving with a menu open.

Your HUD

  • On foot you see your server ID, cash, bank, job and status. In a vehicle you also get speed, gear, fuel, seatbelt, lock, street, compass and postal code.
  • Move it: /hud or M › Misc Settings › Edit HUD Layout. Drag anything where you want it, right-click to hide it, Reset to start over. Your layout is saved.
  • Speedometer style: Classic, Analog dial, Digital, Sport or Minimal in M › Misc Settings › Speedometer (if the server lets players choose).
  • Windshield HUD: drive in first person (V) and your speed, gear, signals and warnings show low on the windshield. Turn it off in M › Misc Settings.

Money

  • You have cash and a bank account. Check them with /cash and /bank, or on the HUD.
  • Use any bank or ATM with E to deposit, withdraw and transfer.
  • Send money to another player's phone number in the phone's Bank app.
  • Jobs pay a paycheck every so often while you're on duty.

Inventory

  • I opens it. Drag items to move them, right-click to use one, and drop items on Use, Give or Drop. Type an amount to split a stack.
  • The first five slots are your hotbar: 1–5 uses them.
  • At a car's trunk, I opens the trunk. Inside a car, it opens the glovebox.
  • Food and drinks keep your hunger and thirst up. You can only carry so much weight.
  • Third eye: hold Left Alt and look at a person, car or object to see what you can do with it, then click.

Vehicles

  • L locks and unlocks a car you have keys for. Give keys to a friend in M › Vehicle Options.
  • Wear your seatbelt (B). Without it, a hard crash throws you through the windshield, and the faster you were going, the more it hurts. You can't get out while you're buckled.
  • Garages: at a garage marker press E: in a car to park it, on foot to take one out. Cars left out may go to the impound after a restart; getting them back costs a fee.
  • Fuel: pull up to a pump and press E. Press G there to buy a jerry can. Electric cars charge at chargers.
  • Weather matters: rain makes the roads slippery, snow and ice more so, and deep water makes you aquaplane. The HUD says Wet roads, Snow on roads or Icy roads.

Voice & radio

  • Talk with your normal push-to-talk key (GTA Settings › Voice Chat).
  • F11 switches between whisper, normal and shout. A circle shows how far you carry.
  • Radio: M › Radio or /radio 12 to join channel 12, /radio 0 to turn it off. Hold Caps Lock to talk; everyone on the channel hears you anywhere on the map. Low channels are often reserved for police and EMS.
  • Phone calls are heard clearly from anywhere.

Homes

  • A green marker at a door means something is for sale. Press E to Look inside, then Buy. Apartment buildings have one apartment per player; a house has one owner.
  • At your door: Go inside and Lock / Unlock. In apartment buildings, Residents lets you ring someone's doorbell; they let you in from M › My Home.
  • M › My Home: storage, wardrobe, visitors, waypoints, Give a key, take keys back, and Sell.
  • Your garage: every home can have one. Park and take out your cars there; key holders can use it too, and each person only sees their own cars.
  • Leaving: at any exit (front door, balcony, back door) pick where you come out: any entrance of the home, or straight to its garage.
  • Your homes appear on the spawn screen, so you can start the day at home. Log off inside and you come back outside the door.

Jobs

  • M › Job (or /duty) puts you on or off duty. You're paid while on duty.
  • The job center in M › Job lists jobs you can take yourself. Others are given by a boss.
  • Bosses get a boss menu (M › Job, or G at the duty point): hire, promote, demote, fire and manage the company's money.
  • /job shows your job and rank.

  • Each service has its own tones: police wail, yelp and priority; EMS hi-lo and phaser; fire the mechanical wail.
  • The PIT timer counts the pursuit and turns green with PIT AUTHORIZED when your department allows it. Your department's command sets the rules.
  • M › Emergency has the speed alarm, Run plate and the BOLO list. The plate reader beeps when it sees a BOLO plate.

Getting hurt

  • Depending on the server, you either respawn at a hospital after a short timer, or go down and wait for EMS to revive you before a timer runs out.
  • Call EMS from the phone's 911 app. On-duty EMS get your location.
  • No EMS around? Use the hospital check-in desk (E) for treatment, for a fee.
  • Bleeding out usually costs a hospital bill, and some servers take items.

Weather

  • The weather changes through the day, and the phone's Weather app shows what's coming.
  • Severe weather (thunderstorm, flood, hurricane, tornado, blizzard) comes with a warning banner and a countdown. Outdoor sirens sound for tornadoes and hurricanes.
  • Floods raise the water, tornadoes pick up cars and people, and lightning can hurt you on foot. Get indoors or drive away from the danger zone on the map.

Streaming & video

  • Streamer mode (/streamer) hides what you choose from your screen: chat, names and IDs, money, location, minimap, phone numbers and more.
  • Video mode (/videomode) clears the screen for filming, with letterbox bars, color filters and slow motion.
  • Free camera (/freecam): fly the camera near your character. Save shot A and shot B and play a smooth move between them.
  • Recorder (/record): records just the game picture, without your HUD or menus. Clips appear in My Clips (/clips), where you can play, trim, add slow motion, join clips and download them. Clips have no sound.

Getting help

  • M › Help & Reports: ask for an admin, report a player, or send a question or bug report. Staff can see your location and claim your request.
  • Quick versions: /calladmin <reason> and /report <message>.
  • Stuck in a menu? Esc closes it, X cancels an action.
